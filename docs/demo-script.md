# AuthScript Demo Script (~2 min)

## Opening (10s)
"Let me show you what AuthScript looks like from inside a doctor's workflow. I'm in an EHR — this is a patient encounter for Rebecca Sandbox."

## Pre-Check / Policy Intelligence (20s)
"The doctor has ordered an MRI Lumbar Spine. Before she even signs the encounter, AuthScript has already run a policy pre-check against the payer's coverage criteria. You can see 5 criteria from Aetna's LCD policy — what's met, what's not, and what documentation gaps exist."

## Documentation Gap Assist (20s)
"There's a gap — the note is missing conservative treatment history. AuthScript suggests language to add. The doctor can edit it, accept it, and save it to the chart. This alone saves minutes of back-and-forth."

## Sign & Analyze (25s)
"Now she signs the encounter. AuthScript automatically pulls the clinical data, runs it against the payer's policy, and returns a confidence score — 93% likelihood of approval. Every criterion has an evidence trail the doctor can click through."

## Form Fill & Submit (25s)
"One click to view the completed PA form — AuthScript has filled out the payer's actual form with patient data, diagnosis codes, CPT codes, provider info, and even checked the clinical indication boxes. Another click submits it. The filled form is written back to the patient's chart as a DocumentReference via FHIR."

## Close (20s)
"Today this process takes staff 15-45 minutes per request. AuthScript does it in under 30 seconds, inline, without leaving the EHR. We're starting with imaging PA — a $16B administrative cost problem — and expanding to procedures and medications."
